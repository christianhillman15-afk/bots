# Master Service Agreement (MSA) — Website + Digital Marketing Retainer

> ⚠️ **NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This is a practical starting template. Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before using it with real clients. Bracketed [PLACEHOLDERS] must be filled in.

---

**THIS MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT** ("Agreement") is entered into as of **[DATE]** ("Effective Date") by and between:

**Provider:** [YOUR AGENCY LEGAL NAME], a [STATE] [LLC/Corp] ("Agency", "we", "us")
**Client:** [CLIENT LEGAL NAME], a [STATE] [entity type] ("Client", "you")

Agency and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

---

## 1. Services
1.1 Agency will provide the website design, hosting, maintenance, and digital marketing services described in the selected package in **Exhibit A — Scope & Package** (the "Services").
1.2 Services are limited to what is expressly listed in Exhibit A. Anything outside that scope ("Out-of-Scope Work") requires a separate written change order and additional fees.
1.3 Agency may use third-party platforms and subcontractors (e.g., hosting, CRM, call tracking, review tools) to deliver the Services.

## 2. Term & Renewal
2.1 **Initial Term:** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for **[12] months**.
2.2 **Auto-Renewal:** After the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews on a **month-to-month** basis until terminated under Section 8.
2.3 **Go-Live:** Agency will use commercially reasonable efforts to launch the website within **[14] business days** of receiving all required Client materials and access (see Section 6).

## 3. Fees & Payment
3.1 **Setup Fee:** $[0 / AMOUNT], due on signing.
3.2 **Monthly Fee:** $[AMOUNT]/month, billed in advance on the **[1st]** of each month via automatic charge to the payment method on file (card or ACH via Stripe/GoHighLevel).
3.3 **Authorization:** Client authorizes Agency to automatically charge the payment method on file for all recurring and approved Out-of-Scope fees.
3.4 **Pass-Through Costs:** Certain usage-based costs (e.g., SMS/phone usage, premium third-party tools, paid ad spend) may be billed at cost or at cost plus a reasonable management margin, as noted in Exhibit A.
3.5 **Late / Failed Payment:** If a payment fails, Agency may retry the charge, apply a late fee of $[25] or [1.5%]/month, and **suspend Services** (including taking the website offline) after [7] days' notice until the balance is cured.
3.6 **No Refunds:** Monthly fees are for the month's services and are non-refundable once the period has begun.

## 4. Ownership & Licenses
4.1 **Domain:** The Client owns its domain name at all times.
4.2 **Client Content:** Client owns all logos, photos, text, and materials it provides ("Client Content") and grants Agency a license to use them to perform the Services.
4.3 **Website & Platform:** Unless a separate buyout is agreed in writing, the website, templates, funnels, automations, and underlying platform/account remain the property of Agency and are **licensed to Client only for the duration of this Agreement**. (This is standard for $0/low-upfront retainer builds.)
4.4 **On Termination:** Client retains its domain, Client Content, and its customer/lead data. Agency is not obligated to transfer proprietary templates, automations, or the platform build unless a buyout fee of $[AMOUNT] is paid.
4.5 **Portfolio:** Agency may reference the work and display it in its portfolio and marketing unless Client objects in writing.

## 5. Performance Disclaimer (Read Carefully)
5.1 Agency will perform Services professionally and in line with current best practices.
5.2 **No Guarantee of Results.** Search rankings, lead volume, call volume, and revenue depend on many factors outside Agency's control (search-engine algorithms, competition, seasonality, Client responsiveness, market conditions). **Agency does not and cannot guarantee any specific ranking (including "#1"), lead count, traffic level, or revenue outcome.**
5.3 Any forecasts or examples are illustrative, not promises.

## 6. Client Responsibilities
6.1 Client will promptly provide required materials and access (logo, photos, content, domain/DNS, Google Business Profile, analytics) needed to perform the Services.
6.2 Delays caused by Client's failure to provide materials/approvals do not relieve Client of payment obligations and may extend timelines.
6.3 Client warrants it has the rights to all Client Content it provides and that such content is lawful and non-infringing.

## 7. Accessibility & Compliance
7.1 Agency will build the website targeting **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** accessibility standards and include an accessibility statement.
7.2 Agency will include a privacy policy and terms of use on the website. Client is responsible for the accuracy of business-specific legal disclosures (licensing numbers, warranty terms, etc.).

## 8. Termination
8.1 **For Convenience (after Initial Term):** Either Party may terminate the month-to-month Agreement with **[30] days'** written notice.
8.2 **During Initial Term:** Early termination by Client requires payment of the lesser of (a) remaining months in the Initial Term or (b) an early-termination fee of $[AMOUNT].
8.3 **For Cause:** Either Party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within [10] days of written notice.
8.4 **Effect:** On termination, access to Agency-owned platform/website ends; Client retains domain, Client Content, and lead data per Section 4.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each Party will keep the other's non-public business information confidential and use it only to perform or receive the Services.

## 10. Limitation of Liability
10.1 Agency's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the **fees paid by Client in the [3] months** preceding the claim.
10.2 Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages (including lost profits).

## 11. Indemnification
Client will indemnify Agency against claims arising from Client Content, Client's products/services, or Client's breach of this Agreement.

## 12. General
12.1 **Independent Contractor:** Agency is an independent contractor, not an employee or partner of Client.
12.2 **Governing Law:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [STATE].
12.3 **Dispute Resolution:** The Parties will first attempt good-faith resolution; unresolved disputes will be handled by [mediation/arbitration/courts] in [COUNTY, STATE].
12.4 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement (with Exhibit A) is the entire agreement and supersedes prior discussions. Changes must be in writing and signed.
12.5 **Severability:** If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
