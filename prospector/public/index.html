📖 What do these numbers mean? — customer cheat-sheet
💵 Est. lost / month (the green number)

A rough estimate of how much money this business is probably leaving on the table every month because of their website problem. It's an educated guess, not an exact bill: we look at how busy their area is and how many reviews they have to estimate how many customers search for them online, assume a broken/missing site loses about 1 in 4 of those, and multiply by what one job is worth.
Say it like: "Based on your reviews and how busy your area is, you're probably losing somewhere around $X/month in jobs to competitors who just show up better online — that's what I'd love to help you fix."

🔢 Score (0–100) + 🔥/🟠/🔵/⚪ tier

How good a fit they are for Launch Media. Higher = better lead. 🔥 Hot = call first, ⚪ Cold = lowest priority. It blends three things → their web problem, whether they can afford you, and how busy their market is.

🏷️ Problem tag

No website = nothing online but a Google listing · Social-only = their "site" is just a Facebook/Instagram page (they don't own it, can't rank) · Broken = web address doesn't work / blank / "for sale" page · Weak site = it loads but it's slow, not mobile-friendly, or insecure.

⭐ Reviews / rating

Our stand-in for "are they making real money." Lots of reviews = busy, established, can afford you. It's why a shop with 300 reviews and no website is a goldmine.

📊 Score breakdown (Problem · Affordability · Market)

The three ingredients of the score, each 0–100. Problem = how bad their web situation is · Affordability = how likely they can pay · Market = how big/busy their city is.

🩺 Site health · 💼 typical job value · 🔎 online leads/mo

Site health = a 0–100 grade of their current site (0 = none/broken). Typical job value = what one customer is worth in their trade (a roof ≈ $12k) — great for "one extra job covers months with us." Online leads/mo = rough guess at how many people search for that service in their area monthly.

📈 The 4 stat cards up top

Total leads = businesses found with a web problem · Hot leads = the best ones to call first · No real website = how many have nothing or only social · Est. monthly opportunity = the total $ across your whole list (your pipeline size, not one customer).

⚠️ The $ figures are honest estimates to start a conversation — always say "around" or "roughly," never promise an exact number.
